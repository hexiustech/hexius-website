Patrick Mathieu Founder & Lead Security Consultant
LinkedIn Blog

Patrick is a cybersecurity expert with over 18 years of experience in application security, penetration testing, and offensive security. He has built and led product security and red team teams at Fortune 100 companies, co-founded Hackfest.ca, and advised on the development of Quebec's first provincial cybersecurity policy. A recognized speaker at DEF CON, Black Hat, and DerbyCon, Patrick combines deep technical expertise with operational leadership on every engagement.
